## Recording rules

For every new asset record: file path, creator / rightsholder, source, license and version, date obtained, attribution text, allowed commercial and mobile uses, modification rights, and proof of permission (keep private receipts outside Git; reference them here). Never put stock previews, reference-game screenshots, competitor assets, third-party logos or unreviewed fonts in the bundle. QA screenshots go in pull requests, not the repository.

## Release gate

Before store submission: owner- and player-review the art, confirm every shipped asset and dependency license, check icon and title similarity, and use only Bus-ster's own screenshots and copy in the listing. Check the packaged AAB, not just this ledger.
